Commands guide

Every bot command, who can use it, and what it does.

🎁 Claims & rewards

$claim — In a ticket: shows invite rewards you unlocked. Reply with a number to claim.
Everyone (tickets only)
$mclaim — In a ticket: message-milestone rewards. Reply with a number.
Everyone (tickets only)
$method list / set Name | text / get / del — Unlimited method text library for auto-delivery.
Staff
$inv / $invites — Show tracked invites (Falcon sync).
Everyone

📦 Stock & pay

$mcfa / $stock — Stock count, list, add, clear.
Staff
$pay @user — DM 1 MCFA from stock.
Staff
$salary @user — Staff salary DM (restricted roles).
Owner / Co-Owner role
$custom / $custompay @user|@role — Custom stock + deliver.
Staff
$clear — Clear MCFA stock.
Staff
